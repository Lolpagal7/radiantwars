# Radiant Wars

Live Demo: https://radiantwars.web.app

RadiantWars is a 3D interactive web experience where you step into the role of an alien exploration team studying a massive black hole from a safe distance.

But something goes wrong.
A critical malfunction pulls your ship into the event horizon. Both automatic and manual protection systems fail. The teleportation link to your home solar system collapses.

As a last resort, an emergency safety protocol activates — flinging your ship across space to the nearest solar system. When you regain control, you find yourself staring at a small blue planet called Earth...

From there, your story begins.
Explore the journey through the interactive world of RadiantWars — where every element reacts, moves, and feels alive.

Built with: React + Vite
Development Time: 2.5 hours
